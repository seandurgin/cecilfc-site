Governance

How Cecil Soccer League, Inc. is organized and governed.

Board of Directors

Cecil Soccer League, Inc. is governed by a volunteer Board of Directors under the league's bylaws. The board sets policy, approves budgets, oversees the league's finances and facilities, and elects the league's officers.

Affiliations

Cecil Soccer League is a member of the Maryland State Youth Soccer Association (MSYSA), US Youth Soccer, and the United States Soccer Federation, and follows their rules and policies. All games are played under FIFA Laws of the Game as modified by US Youth Soccer, MSYSA, and Cecil Soccer.

Policies

  • Non-discrimination: the league does not discriminate on the basis of race, ethnic origin, age, religion, sex, or disability in its membership, board, committees, staff, or participants.
  • Bylaws: the league is governed by bylaws adopted by its Board of Directors.
  • Sideline conduct: coaches are expected to control their players and sidelines. Abusive or disrespectful behavior is not tolerated and can lead to suspension or expulsion.
  • Refunds: see the refund policy.
