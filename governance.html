Governance

Board of Directors

Cecil Soccer League, Inc. is governed by a volunteer Board of Directors under the league's bylaws. The board sets policy, approves budgets, oversees the league's finances and facilities, and elects the league's officers.

Affiliations

Cecil Soccer League is a member of the Maryland State Youth Soccer Association (MSYSA), US Youth Soccer, and the United States Soccer Federation, and follows their rules and policies. All players, coaches, and teams are registered through MSYSA.

Policies

  • Non-discrimination: the league does not discriminate on the basis of race, ethnic origin, age, religion, sex, or disability in its membership, board, committees, staff, or participants.
  • Bylaws: the league is governed by bylaws adopted by its Board of Directors.
  • Refunds: refund requests are made in writing to info@cecilsoccer.org, as described on cecilsoccer.org.
