Programs

Recreational soccer

Spring and fall recreational seasons for players of all skill levels, from first-time players up through high school age. Teams are formed by the league, coached by parent volunteers, and play at the Chesland Soccer Complex. The focus is fun, fundamentals, and sportsmanship.

Registration and season details at cecilsoccer.org

Competitive (travel) soccer

Cecil FC competitive teams are organized by birth year for players looking for a higher level of play. Players are selected through annual tryouts, and teams compete in regional leagues and tournaments. The league registers its competitive players with the Maryland State Youth Soccer Association.

Travel teams and tryout information

Winter indoor

An off-season indoor program played at the Cecil Arena, 2706 North East Rd, North East, MD 21901.

Tournaments, camps, and scholarships

  • Tournaments: the league hosts tournaments at Chesland, including the Chesapeake Cup and the Fall Blast.
  • Camps and clinics: training opportunities for players and coaches.
  • John Jones Memorial College Scholarship: the league’s college scholarship program.

Coaches and referees

All coaches are volunteers. The league supports coach education and licensing and encourages older players to become certified referees.
