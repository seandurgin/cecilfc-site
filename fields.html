Fields

Chesland Soccer Complex

401 Elk Forest Rd, Elkton, MD 21921

The Chesland Soccer Complex is the league's home field and physical location. It sits within Cecil County's Chesland Park. Under a 2002 lease and management agreement with Cecil County, Cecil Soccer League plans, maintains, and operates the soccer facilities on about 88 acres of the park.

The complex has full-size 11v11 fields and smaller fields for younger age groups, plus parking and a playground.
