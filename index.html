Who are we?

We are an established soccer league located in Cecil County, Maryland. Whether you are interested in a Recreational, Competitive and/or Winter (indoor) program, we would love for your family to become part of our family!

Cecil Soccer League, Inc. is a volunteer-run, 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Our programs play as Cecil FC, serving roughly 1,200 players ages 4 to 18 each year at the Chesland Soccer Complex in Elkton.

John Jones Memorial College Scholarship

John Jones was instrumental in building the Chesland Soccer Complex and the Bard Cameron Sports Park. Each year Cecil Soccer awards two $1,500 scholarships in his name to graduating seniors who best exemplify community service, academic excellence, and good sportsmanship.

Where we play

Outdoor rec and home travel games are played at the Chesland Soccer Complex, 401 Elk Forest Rd, Elkton. The league has operated the complex under a lease with Cecil County since 2002. Winter indoor games are at the Cecil Arena in North East.
