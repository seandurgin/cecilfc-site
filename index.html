Who we are

Cecil Soccer League, Inc. is a nonprofit youth soccer organization serving Cecil County, Maryland, and the surrounding area. Our programs play as Cecil FC. We run spring, fall, and winter indoor seasons of instructional youth soccer for players ages 4 to 18, with approximately 1,200 participants each year.

The league is run by volunteer officers, directors, and coaches, and is recognized by the IRS as a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization.

Where we play

Our home is the Chesland Soccer Complex at 401 Elk Forest Rd in Elkton, part of Cecil County's Chesland Park. The league has managed its soccer facilities under a lease with Cecil County since 2002.
